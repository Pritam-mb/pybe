# PyBe — Inheritance Discovery Engine

## Product Specification

### Contribution by Pritam Patra

---

## 1. Product Vision

The **Inheritance Discovery Engine** is an interactive learning experience designed to help learners understand Object-Oriented Inheritance through exploration.

Rather than presenting inheritance as a definition to memorize, the product allows the learner to discover the relationship through a realistic case study and then connect that discovery to Python.

### Core principle

> **Experience the pattern first. Name the concept later.**

The product transforms a programming lesson into a guided investigation.

---

# 2. Problem

Beginners often encounter Object-Oriented Programming through abstract definitions such as:

> A child class inherits properties and methods from a parent class.

Although technically correct, this definition can be difficult for a beginner to visualize.

The product addresses this problem by introducing the relationship through a familiar scenario.

Instead of asking the learner to memorize the meaning of inheritance, the experience asks them to:

- Observe
- Compare
- Identify patterns
- Explain relationships
- Connect those relationships to programming
- Apply the concept in Python

---

# 3. Target Audience

The experience is designed primarily for:

- Beginner Python learners
- Students learning Object-Oriented Programming
- Learners who understand basic Python syntax but are new to OOP
- Students who benefit from visual and scenario-based explanations

No advanced Object-Oriented Programming knowledge is required.

---

# 4. Learning Objectives

After completing the experience, learners should be able to:

1. Recognize an inheritance relationship.
2. Distinguish general characteristics from specialized characteristics.
3. Explain the idea of inheritance in their own words.
4. Connect a real-world relationship to an OOP relationship.
5. Recognize inheritance syntax in Python.
6. Write a basic inherited class.
7. Apply the concept to a new example.
